Works with your assistant

Connect your AI to your degree plan.

Your assistant will be able to read the plan you built here, answer questions about your actual degree — what's left, what you can take next term, what happens if you drop something — and make changes to it when you ask. It takes about a minute to set up, with nothing to install.

You will need an account. Your assistant reads the plan saved to your LionsDegree account, so step one is signing in — and you can disconnect it again at any time.

  1. Sign in to LionsDegree

    Use the Google or Microsoft button with your Penn State account, and build your plan if you have not already. Your assistant reads the plan saved to your account, so there has to be one — you will sign in once more in a moment, to tell your assistant which account is yours.

    Open the planner

  2. Copy your connection link

    This is the address your assistant needs. It is the same for everyone — signing in is what makes it yours.

    https://mcp.lionsdegree.com/mcp

  3. Paste it into your assistant's settings

    Every assistant words this slightly differently. Look for whichever of these it uses:

    • Claude — Customize, then Connectors, then Add, then Add custom connector. Works on every plan; the free plan allows one connector.
    • ChatGPT — Plugins, then the plus button, then Add custom MCP server. This needs a Business, Enterprise or Edu account: on Free and Plus the option is not there, and on Pro it can read your plan but not change it.
    • Something else — look for "Connectors", "Plugins", "Integrations", or "MCP". Paste the link into the address or URL box.

  4. Sign in when it asks

    Your assistant will open a sign-in window — Google or Microsoft, the same one you used on the planner. Sign in there and you're connected. You can disconnect from your assistant's settings screen at any time, and your plan stays where it is.

Then ask it something

Once it's connected, these are the kinds of things it will answer:

  • "What do I still need for my major?"
  • "Can I take CMPSC 465 next spring?"
  • "What would my plan look like if I studied abroad in fall 2027?"
  • "Show me what dropping this course would do before you do it."
  • "Find me a 3-credit elective that also counts for a Gen Ed."

Your assistant can change your plan when you ask it to, and it can show you what a change would do before making it. It will not remove a course your degree requires unless you say so in your own words — and it has to repeat those words back to you.
