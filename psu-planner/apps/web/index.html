Your degree is a route.

Stations are courses. Lines are prerequisites. Pick your majors, minors and certificates, and LionsDegree lays out every semester between here and graduation — holding every prerequisite, staying under the credit cap, and telling you what breaks when you move something.

Free. No account. Your plan stays in your browser.

Pick your programs

Majors, minors and certificates, read from the Penn State bulletin itself — 759 programs across every campus. Add a second major and the plan folds it in, double-counting what genuinely double-counts.

Move a course, see what breaks

Drag CMPSC 465 a semester earlier and the line to CMPSC 360 turns red, because you cannot take it yet. Prerequisites are read from the catalog text and evaluated against your plan, not assumed from the suggested sequence — which is itself sometimes wrong.

Bring your own AI

There is no chatbot to pay for here. Export your plan, point your own assistant at it, and ask it anything — it reads the plan and the catalog locally, on your machine. 

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "lionsdegree": {
      "command": "npx",
      "args": ["-y", "lionsdegree-mcp", "--plan", "/path/to/your-plan.json"]
    }
  }
}

Claude Desktop reads claude_desktop_config.json; Claude Code reads .mcp.json. Export your plan first with Menu → Save plan to a file. It needs no account, no key and no network.

What it can't do

  • It does not know which semesters a course actually runs. Check LionPATH before you rely on a plan.
  • Some requirements name no course list at all — the bulletin says to ask your adviser. Those say so rather than guessing.
  • Where the catalog could not be read cleanly, it says it could not be read. It will not invent a course list to fill a gap.
  • It is not an audit. LionPATH and your adviser decide what counts.

Unofficial. LionsDegree is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of The Pennsylvania State University. Course data is scraped from the public bulletin and may be out of date or wrong. Verify everything with your academic adviser and LionPATH before you register.
