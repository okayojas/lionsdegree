What this isn't

Not affiliated with Penn State. Not a substitute for your adviser. It cannot see when a class actually runs — check LionPATH. If a requirement cannot be verified, it says so instead of guessing.

Measured across %COVERAGE_PROGRAMS% programs in the %COVERAGE_EDITION% bulletin, not estimated. %COVERAGE_NAMED% requirements name their courses outright, and %COVERAGE_PATTERNED% give you a subject and a level to choose within. %COVERAGE_UNREADABLE% we could not read at all, and those are flagged for you rather than guessed at. %COVERAGE_GENED% are General Education buckets, filled from the designations a course carries.

Left out of that percentage altogether: the %COVERAGE_ADVISER% requirements where the Bulletin publishes no list and tells you to ask your adviser. Those are not failures to read something — there is nothing there to read — so the planner shows you the requirement's own wording instead.
